About Evox

Real Transformation Always Starts from Within

At Evox we design experiences that move people. We blend adventure and wellness to reconnect teams and individuals with their bodies, their essence, and nature. Through hiking, paddle and kayak outings, via ferrata, retreats, fitness, physiotherapy, and mindfulness, we turn challenge into clarity, trust, and lasting growth. FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your journey with Axeris.

Is this only for professional athletes?

Not at all. Axeris is designed for anyone who wants to understand their mindset — from beginners to elite competitors. Everyone can gain insight into how they approach training, motivation, and challenges.

How long does it take to get results?

The scan is brief and intuitive. Insights are ready instantly, so you can start applying them right away.

Do I need any special knowledge?

No prior experience or psychology background is needed. Axeris is built to be human-first, with clear, practical insights anyone can understand.

Can I track my progress over time?

Yes. Re-scan anytime to monitor growth in mindset, motivation, and confidence. Visual dashboards help you see trends and strengths.

Is my data secure and private?

100%. Your results are encrypted, securely stored, and only accessible to you. Your personal information and scan data are never shared without consent.

How often should I use Axeris?

Regular scans help maintain awareness of your mental performance. Tracking over weeks and months allows you to measure growth, refine strategies, and stay consistently on track.
